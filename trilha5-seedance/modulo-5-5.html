Build me an award-winning cinematic "3D scroll" PERSONAL PORTFOLIO website for me — [YOUR NAME], [WHAT YOU DO IN ONE LINE].

VISUALS — seedance_2_5 on the Higgsfield MCP, mode omni_reference, 720p, 16:9, generate_audio false. First upload my attached photo to Higgsfield and save it as a reference element, then pass it in image_references on EVERY generation so my face stays consistent. Keep my wardrobe identical throughout: [black t-shirt, dark overshirt]. Generate ONE hero still of me first with GPT Image 2 using that reference, then use it as start_image for the clips.

  1. HERO (duration 12) — "the camera orbits slowly around the man standing still and composed in a black-void studio,

[ACCENT COLOUR] rim light tracing his profile, fine particles drifting, one continuous shot, no cuts, he stays perfectly still."

  1. THE WORK (duration 10) — "slow cinematic push-in on the man seated at a dark desk surrounded by floating holographic screens, screens glowing, one continuous shot."
  2. THE CLOSE (duration 8) — "the man walks slowly toward camera down a dark gallery lined with glowing screens, stopping in a hero pose, one continuous shot."

WEBSITE — scroll-scrub the hero orbit so scrolling turns me, with "[YOUR NAME]" in massive display type tracking in letter by letter. Then: an animated stats strip counting up on scroll ([your 3-4 best numbers]); a THREE PILLARS section over clip 2 revealing [your three offers] one at a time; a WORK grid over clip 3 with cards for [your three best projects], each with a one-line pitch and hover motion; a finale with your main CTA and footer links to [your socials]. A chapter rail down the left edge, a live frame counter in the corner, custom cursor, film grain. Design: ink black, [ACCENT COLOUR] accent, cream type, bold condensed display, Lenis smooth scroll. Put the hero copy in the lower- left over a dark gradient scrim — never centred on top of my face. Launch on localhost and verify the orbit scrub is buttery before telling me it's done.

Faça-o seu: tudo entre colchetes é um campo para preencher. Gere duas ou três tomadas apenas da órbita do herói e mantenha aquela em que sua semelhança se mantém consistente durante toda a rotação — sua foto de entrada decide tudo.

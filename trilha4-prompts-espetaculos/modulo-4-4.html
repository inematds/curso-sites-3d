THE PRODUCT (fictional jewelry house): AURELIA, a solitaire ring in 24 karat gold. Tagline: "From fire." The site's hero moment is CREATION, not destruction: a ribbon of molten gold pours through darkness, coils itself into a spinning band, cools from white heat through ember orange to polished gold, and a diamond descends into its setting with a single flare. The visitor scrolls the ring into existence.

THE TECHNIQUE (follow exactly): The 3D scroll effect is NOT Three.js. It is a canvas image-sequence scrub: video clips split into frames, scroll position drives which frame paints onto a full-viewport canvas.

ASSETS (Higgsfield connector): 1. ONE 16:9 anchor image: "A luminous ribbon of molten gold suspended mid- pour in a pure black void, white hot core fading to deep ember orange at the edges, tiny sparks drifting, macro jewelry photography, photorealistic, dramatic, no text, no watermark." Best image model in the catalog. 2. With the anchor as image reference on every clip, three 10-second 1080p 16:9 image-to-video clips: CLIP A "the pour": molten gold pours slowly through the black void, coiling and folding, sparks rising, hypnotic and continuous. CLIP B "the forming": the molten stream wraps into a spinning ring shape, the glow cooling from white through orange to warm polished gold, surface tension smoothing to a mirror finish, then a round diamond descends from above and seats into the setting with one bright flare, ending on the finished ring rotating slowly. CLIP C "the macro": extreme macro orbit of the finished ring, engraving appearing on the inner band reading AURELIA, rainbow fire flashing inside the diamond. 3. Download the MP4s.
